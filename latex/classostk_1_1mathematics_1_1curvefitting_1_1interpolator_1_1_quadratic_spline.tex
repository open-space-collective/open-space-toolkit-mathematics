\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\mbox{\hyperlink{classostk_1_1mathematics_1_1curvefitting_1_1interpolator_1_1_quadratic_spline}{Quadratic\+Spline}}. 

A quadratic spline is a piecewise second-\/degree polynomial fitted so that it passes through the given data points. The resulting interpolant is C1 continuous, and reproduces quadratic polynomials exactly. It is cheaper and less oscillatory than a cubic spline, at the cost of a lower order of accuracy.

The derivatives at the two end points are estimated from the data.

Nodes may be uniformly or arbitrarily spaced. Uniformly spaced nodes are interpolated with a cardinal B-\/spline, which finds the containing interval in constant time rather than searching for it, and is therefore faster to evaluate. Which of the two is used is decided from the data and is not otherwise observable.

Use when the data is smooth and only values are known, and a continuous first derivative is enough. It is cheaper and less oscillatory than a cubic spline.
