\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\mbox{\hyperlink{classostk_1_1mathematics_1_1curvefitting_1_1interpolator_1_1_cubic_hermite}{Cubic\+Hermite}}. 

A cubic Hermite spline is a piecewise third-\/degree polynomial which matches both the value and the first derivative of the underlying function at every node. The resulting interpolant is C1 continuous.

Nodes may be uniformly or arbitrarily spaced. Uniformly spaced nodes are interpolated with a cardinal Hermite spline, which finds the containing interval in constant time rather than searching for it, and is therefore faster to evaluate. Which of the two is used is decided from the data and is not otherwise observable.

Use when the first derivative is known at every sample, for instance velocity alongside position. The derivatives are honoured exactly, and each interval depends only on its two end nodes.
